Privacy Policy

—

Your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains how Prasiacap handles information while you use our application, in full compliance with applicable data protection laws.

Table of Contents

1. Introduction

Prasiacap ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to transparency regarding information practices. This policy governs your use of our mobile application (the "App"), available on iOS. By downloading, accessing, or using the App, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • No personally identifiable information (PII) — We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, postal addresses, or any data that can be used to directly identify you.
  • No usage analytics — We do not employ analytics SDKs, crash-reporting services, or tracking pixels. We do not log which features you use or how frequently.
  • No advertising identifiers — We do not access or transmit advertising IDs (IDFA/AAID), nor do we participate in ad-targeting or attribution networks.
  • No location data — The App does not request, access, or transmit GPS coordinates, IP-based location, or any geolocation information.
  • No biometric data — We do not collect, store, or process Face ID, Touch ID, fingerprints, or any other biometric templates.
  • No financial information — We do not collect payment details, banking information, or any financial transaction records through the App itself.

3. Information Collected (If Any)

Certain system-level information may be implicitly transmitted by Apple devices as part of normal network operations (e.g., device model, operating system version, and network type). This information is:

  • Transmitted automatically by the iOS framework, not by our application code
  • Used solely for maintaining compatibility and service functionality
  • Not linked to any personal identifier
  • Not stored beyond the duration necessary for functional purposes
  • Not shared with any third party except where legally compelled

4. Third-Party Services

The App may integrate with optional third-party services (e.g., push notification providers, cloud backup) only when explicitly authorized by you through system permission dialogs. Each such service is governed by its own privacy policy, and we encourage you to review them. We do not endorse, control, or assume responsibility for the privacy practices of any third-party provider.

Important: You may revoke any permission at any time through your device's Settings application without affecting the core functionality of the App.

5. Data Security

We employ industry-standard security measures including TLS encryption for any network communication, sandboxing enforced by the iOS operating system, and regular security reviews of our codebase. While no system can guarantee absolute security, we implement technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect any information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to individuals under the age of 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect any information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided information through the App, please contact us immediately, and we will take steps to delete such information.

7. Your Rights (GDPR / CCPA)

Under applicable data protection regulations, including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) for users in the European Economic Area (EEA) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) for residents of California, you may have the following rights:

  • Right of access — Request a copy of any personal data we hold about you
  • Right to rectification — Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Right to erasure — Request deletion of your personal data
  • Right to portability — Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format
  • Right to restrict processing — Request limitation on how we use your data
  • Right to object — Object to processing based on legitimate interests
  • Right to non-discrimination — Exercise your rights without adverse treatment

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at privacy@prasiacap.com. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Cookies & Local Storage

The App may use local storage mechanisms (including Keychain, UserDefaults, and similar iOS-native storage) solely to preserve your preferences and settings within the application. These mechanisms are not used for cross-app tracking, advertising, or any purpose beyond local functionality. No cookies are used in the traditional sense, as the App operates as a native iOS application.

9. Data Retention & Deletion

Any data collected through the App is retained only for as long as necessary to provide the requested service or as required by applicable law. You may request deletion of any stored data at any time by contacting us. Upon deletion, all copies of your information will be permanently removed from our systems within 30 days. Uninstalling the App does not automatically trigger deletion of any server-side data; please contact us separately if you wish to request deletion upon uninstallation.

10. International Data Transfers

If you access the App from a jurisdiction outside the country where our servers are located, your information may be transferred across borders. We ensure that any such transfer complies with applicable data protection laws, including implementing appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses where required.

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our practices, technology, legal requirements, or other factors. We will notify you of material changes by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy and, where required by law, by providing additional notice through the App or other communication channels. Your continued use of the App after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.
