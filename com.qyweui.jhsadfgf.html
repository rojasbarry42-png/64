Privacy Policy Confirmation

Prasiacap is committed to protecting your privacy. This document outlines how the application handles user data and confirms that we do not collect, store, or transmit personal information.

1. Data Collection

Prasiacap does not collect, upload, store, or monitor any user data.

  • We do not access your device's contacts, messages, or files unless explicitly required by the app's core function, which in this case is not applicable.
  • We do not track your location or internet activity.
  • No analytics or third-party tracking scripts are included in this application.

2. Data Usage and Sharing

Because no data is collected, there is no data to use, process, or share with third parties.

3. Data Security

As no personal data is retained on our servers or on your device, there is no risk of data breach or unauthorized access to your private information.

4. User Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. Since we do not hold any personal data, these rights are inherently satisfied. If you believe there is an issue, please contact the developer directly.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be effective immediately upon posting the updated policy on this page.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this privacy policy, please contact the developer of Prasiacap through the channels provided in the app store listing.
